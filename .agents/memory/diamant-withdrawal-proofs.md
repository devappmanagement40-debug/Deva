---
name: DIAMANT withdrawal proofs
description: Rules for member-submitted withdrawal proof posts and their displayed sharing bonuses.
---

Approved withdrawal proofs are shown to other members with the submitter's phone number masked. Admin-assigned sharing bonuses are display-only and do not alter wallet balances or create transactions.

**Why:** The requested bonus is a public display amount, not a payment to the member.

**How to apply:** Keep submissions out of the member feed until approved, mask phone numbers in public responses, and never credit the displayed bonus unless the user explicitly authorizes a separate wallet-credit feature.
