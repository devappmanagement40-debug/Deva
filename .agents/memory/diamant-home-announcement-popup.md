---
name: DIAMANT home announcement popup
description: Visual and interaction rules for the home announcement modal.
---

The home announcement popup uses DIAMANT's violet-and-blue platform palette. Do not show the floating mascot above it. Make the official Telegram channel action prominent and place the Annuler/close control at the top right.

**Why:** the user specified this design direction for the home announcement popup.

**How to apply:** keep announcement copy admin-managed and continue opening the configured official channel link. Do not apply this rule to the wheel invitation and rules popups, which have their own visual reference.
