---
name: Supported country markets
description: The customer-facing market list is Côte d’Ivoire and Togo only; legacy records remain intact.
---

Only Côte d’Ivoire (`CI`) and Togo (`TG`) are supported customer markets. Login and registration must never offer or accept other country codes, including a legacy `CD` row. Country-dependent customer endpoints for deposits, withdrawals, and operators must also expose only active `CI`/`TG` rows. Preserve historical accounts, transactions, and country records in storage; do not delete them merely to hide a market.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires Côte d’Ivoire and Togo to be the only country choices and corrected the RDC appearing on both login and registration. Existing country-linked records must remain intact.

**How to apply:** Keep the supported market-code rule centralized and enforce it in country-list APIs, registration/login schemas, and server-side account creation. Keep country-specific details such as CI Wave and Togo USSD/payment instructions configurable in their existing admin-managed records. Bootstrap and migration seeds must create only CI and TG; changing the visible market list must not delete or rewrite historical database records.

Registration lists active supported markets; login lists configured supported markets, including inactive CI/TG entries so existing members can still sign in. Reject all non-CI/TG registration and login attempts server-side, even if a legacy row is active in the database. Do not delete a country while accounts, payment records, wallets, deposit channels, or receiving numbers still reference it. Product prices and product earnings are entered directly as XOF and displayed as XOF without conversion. Team/referral summaries, member totals, and other visible amount labels throughout the site use XOF without conversion; stored numbers and transaction logic remain unchanged. On the « Moi » profile card only, the raw balance number is labeled FCFA without conversion. The deposit page lists every active supported country configured for manual deposits, regardless of the member's registration country or whether a receiving number is currently configured; load active receiving numbers only after the member chooses a country, and validate the selected country, number, and channel on submission. Do not hardcode operator lists. New crypto deposits accept only USDT BEP20, while existing deposit history and settlements remain intact. Withdrawals offer USDT BEP20 and active Mobile Money operator names configured for the user's country; do not expose deposit receiving numbers as withdrawal destinations.

**Why:** country activation controls customer registration and available payment markets, while account, payment, and withdrawal history must not be erased. The user wants only CI and TG selectable, with country-specific payment methods kept configurable.

**How to apply:** Keep market availability centralized to CI/TG and country-specific operators admin-managed. Refuse deleting a country while stored records reference it. Show visible monetary amounts with XOF using stored numeric values unchanged; apply FCFA only on the « Moi » profile balance. Keep Mobile Money withdrawals limited to operators configured for the user's country and USDT withdrawals on BEP20.
