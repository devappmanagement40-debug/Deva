**Rule:** The receipt mockup applies to withdrawals only. Use solid green for approved/completed withdrawals, solid red for rejected/failed withdrawals, and a distinct brand treatment for pending withdrawals. Keep deposit and earnings histories in their existing layouts. Preserve all transaction amounts and business logic. A rejected withdrawal must not be labeled as received; show its expected net amount instead.

**Why:** The user clarified that the supplied mockup depicts withdrawals, not deposits or earnings. Status wording must also not imply that a rejected payout reached the user.

**How to apply:** Use the mockup styling only on withdrawal-history rows and withdrawal tabs in the shared history views or modal. Do not spread it to deposit or earnings rows.
