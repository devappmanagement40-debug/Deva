Products belong to exactly one category; legacy products marked “all” belong in Stability. Offres launch weekly, have a limited purchase count, and advertise potentially higher returns subject to their individual terms; do not present returns as guaranteed. Parcours products may have their own conditions, including a VIP level. The user describes VIP progression as based on cumulative product investments and investments from filleuls; exact thresholds are to be configured by an administrator. FAQ copy is admin-editable, and the user chose not to change the current VIP calculation.
