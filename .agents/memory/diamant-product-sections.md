Internal product categories remain Stability, Wellness, and Activity, while their French labels are Explore, Parcours, and Offres. Products belong to exactly one category; legacy products marked “all” belong in Stability. Buying Parcours or Offres requires an active Explore holding, including an active admin-assigned holding. Explore catalog cycles are 150 days; existing purchase snapshots stay unchanged. Offres launch weekly, have limited purchase counts, and may have higher returns subject to their terms; never present returns as guaranteed.

VIP0 means no personal paid Parcours purchase. The first such purchase grants at least VIP1; VIP2–VIP7 use cumulative personal paid purchases across Explore, Parcours, and Offres, with no filleul, team, or invitation-count conditions. No product purchase may require invitations. Personal paid purchases made before the first Parcours purchase count toward later thresholds once Parcours activates VIP. Count a purchase as soon as it is successfully recorded; never wait for its product cycle to finish. Investment thresholds and each Parcours product’s minimum required VIP level are configured by an administrator. A member at a higher rank retains access to products requiring lower ranks. Free or admin-assigned products do not count as personal investment. VIP progress restarts from zero toward the next threshold when a rank is reached, while cumulative investment remains intact. Explore products have no VIP rank; the active-Explore purchase prerequisite is separate from VIP.
