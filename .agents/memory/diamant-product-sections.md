Internal product categories remain Stability, Wellness, and Activity, while their French labels are Explore, Parcours, and Offres. Products belong to exactly one category; legacy products marked “all” belong in Stability. Buying Parcours or Offres requires an active Explore holding, including an active admin-assigned holding. Explore catalog cycles are 150 days; active and future Explore purchases credit gains after each complete 24-hour period, with unpaid accrued gains on active purchases released once at rollout and no additional end-cycle payout. Parcours and Offres remain end-cycle. This payout change does not rewrite purchase snapshots. Offres launch weekly, have limited purchase counts, and may have higher returns subject to their terms; never present returns as guaranteed.

VIP0–VIP7 are determined by admin-configured cumulative personal paid-investment thresholds, including VIP1. Purchases across Explore, Parcours, and Offres count immediately once recorded, including purchases made before any Parcours purchase; cycle completion is not required. No filleul, team, or invitation-count conditions apply. No product purchase may require invitations. Free or admin-assigned products do not count as personal investment. Thresholds are positive, contiguous, increasing USDT amounts; a missing threshold prevents that rank and higher ranks from unlocking. To require an investment amount before a Parcours product, configure its minimum VIP level and configure that rank’s USDT threshold; the account progress bar uses the same cumulative purchase total and threshold. Each Parcours product’s minimum required VIP level remains separately configurable, and higher ranks retain access to lower-rank products. The account/Moi VIP card displays the amount remaining to the next configured rank, including VIP0→VIP1. Customer product cards do not display VIP rank or investment thresholds; the server still enforces the per-product VIP requirement. VIP progress restarts from zero toward the next threshold when a rank is reached, while cumulative investment remains intact. Explore products have no VIP rank; the active-Explore purchase prerequisite is separate from VIP.

Product cards retain catalog order, not VIP-rank order. Newly added products are appended after existing products; equal sort positions use creation order as a stable tie-breaker.
