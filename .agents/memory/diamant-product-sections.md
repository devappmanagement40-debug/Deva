Internal product categories remain Stability, Wellness, and Activity, while their French labels are Explore, Parcours, and Offres. Products belong to exactly one category; legacy products marked “all” belong in Stability. Buying Parcours or Offres requires an active Explore holding, including an active admin-assigned holding. Explore catalog cycles are 150 days; active and future Explore purchases credit gains after each complete 24-hour period, with unpaid accrued gains on active purchases released once at rollout and no additional end-cycle payout. Parcours and Offres remain end-cycle. This payout change does not rewrite purchase snapshots. Offres launch weekly, have limited purchase counts, and may have higher returns subject to their terms; never present returns as guaranteed.

VIP ranks are awarded only by personal paid Parcours purchases, following the product catalog order: the first Parcours purchase awards VIP1 without requiring a prior VIP rank; later products award the corresponding rank, up to VIP7. Personal paid USDT purchases across Explore, Parcours, and Offres count toward each Parcours product’s own admin-configured cumulative investment requirement, but Explore and Offres never award VIP. Free and admin-assigned products do not count. Parcours products after the first require the preceding VIP rank and may impose a higher configured minimum. An active Explore product remains a separate prerequisite for Parcours and Offres. The account progress bar tracks cumulative personal investment against the next Parcours product’s requirement. Customer product cards do not display VIP rank or investment thresholds; explain requirements in the purchase flow and enforce them in server storage. Do not use legacy global `vipNMinInvestmentUsdt` settings for rank awards or Parcours purchase requirements.

Product cards retain catalog order, not VIP-rank order. Newly added products are appended after existing products; equal sort positions use creation order as a stable tie-breaker.

FAQ copy remains admin-editable.
