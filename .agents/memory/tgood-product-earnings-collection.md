---
name: TGOOD product earnings collection
description: Product gains are credited automatically at the end of the full cycle for existing and future purchases; manual collection is disabled.
---

Product earnings accrue during the full configured cycle, then are credited automatically to `totalEarnings` once; the deposit balance is never used for this payout. New purchases start with the full cycle remaining and no immediately available gain. Existing purchases keep their progress, and only unpaid legacy earnings are settled so previously credited gains are not paid twice. There is no manual collection in the interface or API. The Solde page shows balance and earnings aggregates, never product cards or a pending collection amount. Product cards and cycle progress remain in the product-history screens. « Mes commandes » is the canonical purchased-products page and also serves as « Mes produits »; both entry points should open the same screen. On the homepage, « Revenu total » includes product gains only after they have been credited, not while the cycle is still running. In « Commande », show a purchased product as « Actif » only while it is active and has days remaining; expired products belong in « Terminés », and both categories remain in purchase history.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose automatic credit at the end of the full product cycle for both existing and future purchases and said there should be no manual collection. They also clarified that « Mes commandes » and « Mes produits » are the same purchased-products screen. Previously collected earnings must remain untouched.

**How to apply:** Start new purchases at the full cycle duration, accrue in complete 24-hour periods, and credit the outstanding amount atomically at cycle end. For completed legacy purchases, pay only the unpaid amount and never repeat prior credits. Keep « Revenu total » limited to credited gains, keep the Solde page to aggregates, and route both « Mes commandes » and « Mes produits » to the same purchased-products history, classifying products with no remaining days as completed without removing them from history.
