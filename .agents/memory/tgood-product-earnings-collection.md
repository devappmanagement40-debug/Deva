In the current DIAMANT policy, Explore gains are credited automatically after each full 24-hour period for active and future purchases; any accrued-but-unpaid gains on active Explore purchases are released once at rollout, with no extra end-cycle payment. Parcours and Offres are credited at cycle end. All gains credit `totalEarnings`, never the deposit balance. Previously credited gains must not be paid twice, and there is no manual collection in the interface or API. The Solde page shows balance and earnings aggregates, never product cards or a pending collection amount. Product cards and cycle progress remain in the product-history screens. « Mes commandes » is the canonical purchased-products page and also serves as « Mes produits »; both entry points should open the same screen. On the homepage, « Revenu total » includes product gains only after they have been credited, not while the cycle is still running. In « Commande », show a purchased product as « Actif » only while it is active and has days remaining; expired products belong in « Terminés », and both categories remain in purchase history.

**Why:** The earlier TGOOD rule was cycle-end payout for all products. On October 10, 2026, the user set the current DIAMANT category rule: Explore pays after every full 24 hours for active and future purchases, while Parcours and Offres remain cycle-end. There is still no manual collection, and previously credited earnings must remain untouched.

**How to apply:** Start new purchases at the full cycle duration and preserve snapshots. For Explore, credit each full elapsed 24-hour period and release only unpaid accrued earnings once for active purchases; do not issue a second cycle-end payment. For Parcours and Offres, credit the outstanding amount atomically at cycle end. Keep « Revenu total » limited to credited gains, keep the Solde page to aggregates, and route both « Mes commandes » and « Mes produits » to the same purchased-products history, classifying products with no remaining days as completed without removing them from history.
